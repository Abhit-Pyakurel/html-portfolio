About Me

I am Abhit, a Customer-Centered Growth Strategist. I help organizations understand what their customers are trying to solve and turn those insights into meaningful business growth. My work brings together customer research, SEO, content, messaging, storytelling, analytics, experimentation, and AI-powered growth systems. I believe that successful growth begins with understanding people: their needs, challenges, questions, and motivations. My philosophy is simple: understand people, create value, earn trust, build relationships, and create growth. Rather than treating SEO, content writing, social media, or AI as separate services, I see them as tools that help businesses understand their customers better and turn that understanding into measurable results.

With more than 25 years of experience working with people, communication, behavior, education, storytelling, and transformation, I bring a human-centered perspective to modern marketing. I combine this experience with SEO, analytics, content strategy, artificial intelligence, and growth experimentation. I am particularly interested in helping startups, technology companies, and mission-driven organizations navigate growth challenges. My approach combines human judgment and creativity with AI-powered research and automation. AI can handle repetitive research and information gathering, allowing me to focus on what matters most: understanding context, building relationships, making strategic decisions, and creating meaningful solutions. I believe that the best growth opportunities emerge when businesses listen carefully to their customers and transform what they learn into better products, clearer messaging, useful content, and stronger customer experiences.

My current focus is building a growth marketing practice around real customer problems and measurable outcomes. Instead of simply offering marketing services, I look for organizations that are experiencing specific challenges or actively seeking growth support. I research their needs, understand their situations, start meaningful conversations, and explore opportunities for small, focused paid experiments. These experiments may involve customer intelligence, demand discovery, SEO opportunities, content strategy, or growth initiatives. My goal is not simply to generate traffic, followers, or marketing activity, but to help businesses discover what works, measure results, and continuously improve. I believe in learning through action rather than making assumptions. Every conversation, experiment, and customer insight is an opportunity to create value. Ultimately, I want to help organizations grow by connecting human understanding, strategic thinking, creative storytelling, and the intelligent use of AI.
